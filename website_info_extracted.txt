The Indian economy is based on agriculture. 60% population depends upon farming. Most of the cultivated land depends upon rain water due to which there is less productivity. Indian farming having non advanced technology causes helplessness in farmers resulting and committing suicides by farmers.
Advanced farming and increase in production can only make the farmers happy. Keeping in view of the same, “Lokmangal Group of Industries” has been established. First of all “Lokmangal co-operative Bank ltd.” Has been established being source for developing the common man and farmer. After that the Group established a sugar factory named “Lokmangal Agro Industries Ltd”.
The main intension behind raising the factory was to make easily available source of market for near-by farmers’ sugarcane. The Group made its vision clear by establishing a milk dairy near the sugar factory. Lokmangal Dairy Products Ltd.”  became very popular and easy market source for farmers’ milk.
Thereafter the Group started running Horticulture College (4 years degree) course and school of Agriculture for the needy students. The aim of running the courses was again to improve agriculture.
Beside all above achievements the Group had established an advance technology based “Lokmangal Distillery and 70 Mega watt Ethenol Co-generation Plant”, recently.
Keeping in view need of the farmers in broad vision, Lokmangal had established “Maharashtra Research and Development Centre”. Near sugar factory. The institution is having well equipped and advanced laboratory in which qualified and skilled staff is working. The laboratory provides free soil and water testing facilities to the users.
The Group had established “Lokmangal Bio-Tech Pvt. Ltd.” This became backbone of high yielding technique. Company has proved tremendous increase in production based on the concept “balance nutrition” and its actual application in the farm.
The farmers became economically sound due to increase in production by approximately 30% by using organic fertilizers, bio-fertilizers, chemical fertilizers and essential micronutrients.
